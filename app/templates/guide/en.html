Getting started

In one minute

  1. Get a key. Create an account: {{jours_essai}} days and {{validations_essai}} free validations, no credit card. The key (sk_live_…) is shown only once. After that, it can be regenerated from your dashboard.
  2. Call POST /validate with the key in the X-API-Key header.
  3. Read the result: standardized address, deliverability, coordinates, and cache_key.

In every example, replace sk_live_YOUR_KEY with your key.

No account yet? Test an address for free: the page stores nothing and shows you exactly what the API returns.

Access

Authentication

Two ways in, one response. Only the header you send and the payment differ.

Subscription

An online store

You send
X-API-Key: sk_live_…, the key shown at signup, or regenerated in your dashboard.
You pay
The monthly quota of your plan. Nothing is charged per call.

Pay per call · x402

An autonomous agent

You send
X-PAYMENT: a signed EIP-3009 authorization, built from the body of the 402 response (accepts field).
You pay
{{prix_x402}} USDC per validation charged.
The key takes priority. If both headers are sent together, the subscription wins: a call covered by an account is never charged in USDC. Each response says how it was paid, in X-Payment-Mode.

Never put the key in a git repository, a shared file or a URL. It lives in an environment variable or a secrets manager, and only travels over https. A leaked key is regenerated in one click: the old one stops working immediately.

The contract

Request

POST {{base}}/validate, JSON body, content-type: application/json header.

Response 200

FieldWhat it says
validated
The provider recognized the address. false is not always a verdict: read degraded before drawing a conclusion.
deliverability
residential, commercial, unknown or invalid: the provider's code, translated into plain words.
confidence
A score between 0 and 1: a score, not a guarantee. Choose your threshold based on the return rate you accept.
degraded
true: the provider did not answer (outage, timeout, open circuit breaker). The response says nothing about the address. Never record it as an "invalid address".
price_paid
0.0 for a call covered by a subscription; the price in USDC for a paid call.
cache_key
Pseudonym of the address (SHA-256). It is used for the cache and for any deletion request. Keep it: it does not contain the address.

Response headers

These are the headers the route actually sets. No others are promised.

HeaderContent
X-Payment-Mode
api_key or x402: how the call was paid.
X-Plan
trial, starter, pro or enterprise. Key mode only.
X-Quota-Remaining
Validations remaining this month. Absent: unlimited plan, the absence means "no cap".

Eight possible codes on /validate. The three key-mode rejections (401, 402, 403) are distinct on purpose: handle each in its own way.

  1. 200Validation served

    Success

    Read result. Keep cache_key: it is your pseudonym for this address and is used to request deletion.

    Nothing to retry

  2. 401Unknown API key

    Fix on your side

    The key is incomplete, revoked (regenerated) or meant for another service. Fix the environment variable. Do not retry in a loop.

    No: fix the key

    {"x402Version": {{x402_version}}}Payment is missing for this call. The price is in the accepts field.

  4. 403Account expired

    Fix on your side

    The free trial is over and the account is still on the trial plan. Move to a paid plan: nothing is charged automatically.

    No: change plan

    Fix on your side

    A field is missing or empty. street, city, state and zip are required; country defaults to US.

    After fixing it

    Limit

    Applies to /validate-form, the trial without an account, not the paid API. Wait for the delay given in Retry-After, or create an account.

    Service-side incident

    The service refuses to serve a validation it cannot record. Try again. If the call was paid, quote transaction_id: reconciliation is done by hand.

    Yes, keep transaction_id

    Service-side incident

    The key could not be verified (this is not a rejection), or the database is not responding. Try again in a few minutes: nothing was consumed.

Billing

What the cache reuses, and what is capped

TopicWhat happensWhat you do about it
Cache (verdict)
The same normalized address does not query the provider again for {{cache_jours}} days. cache_key guarantees this.
To doA repeated check is answered faster, but still counts as a check. To force a new call, change the address: the API does not guess.
Cache (degraded)
A degraded result is kept for {{cache_minutes}} minutes, not {{cache_jours}} days: "the provider did not answer" is not a verdict.
To doTreat degraded: true as "try again later". After this short window, the same call gets a real answer.
Quota
Monthly, on the UTC calendar month: it resets on the 1st of the month, not 30 days after your first call.
To doWatch X-Quota-Remaining. Write to us before the limit rather than after.
Price per call
{{prix_x402}} USDC per validation charged (x402 mode). A call covered by a subscription reports price_paid: 0.0.
To doA paying agent reads the 402 body (accepts): the price is there, never to be guessed.
Trial without an account
{{essai_sans_compte}} validations per day per visitor on /validate-form.
To doFor regular use, create an account: {{validations_essai}} free validations, then a monthly plan.

3. Shopify

StepWhat you connect
Trigger
An orders/create webhook (or your package-preparation step) calls your service, which queries /validate with the order's shipping address.
Field mapping
  • shipping_address.address1 → street (add address2 to line2 on the result side)
  • city → city
  • province_code → state
  • zip → zip
  • country_code → country
Decision
validated: false: flag the order in your back office before shipping. degraded: true: block nothing, schedule another check.

4. WooCommerce

StepWhat you connect
Trigger
The woocommerce_checkout_order_processed action (order paid), or a cron job that goes through orders being prepared (wc_get_orders( ['status' => 'processing'] )).
Field mapping
  • $order->get_shipping_address_1() → street
  • $order->get_shipping_city() → city
  • $order->get_shipping_state() → state
  • $order->get_shipping_postcode() → zip
  • $order->get_shipping_country() → country
Traceability
Store cache_key with the order: it is the only address identifier valid for a deletion request.
What we do not provide
No WooCommerce extension: the call comes from your service.

In every case: one call per address and per order, never in a loop that ignores the return codes. A quota 402 is not an outage: it is a limit, and it is read from the response.

Developers and AI agents

Pay per call, no account

An autonomous agent needs neither an account nor a key. Each validation charged costs {{prix_x402}} USDC, paid with x402 on the Base network.

  1. 1Call POST /validate with the usual body. Without payment, the response is a 402 whose body carries {"x402Version": {{x402_version}}} and the accepts field: the price is there.
  2. 2Sign the authorization. Build an EIP-3009 authorization from accepts, and resend the request with the X-PAYMENT header.
  3. 3Read the response. X-Payment-Mode is x402, price_paid gives the price in USDC, and transaction_id is the reference to quote in case of a 500.

Good to know before you integrate

Limits

United States Optimized
The provider is optimized for United States addresses: standardized address, coordinates, deliverability and confidence score.
France and overseas Verified (BAN)
French and overseas addresses are checked against the Base Adresse Nationale: score, postcode and city must match. Other countries remain partially verified.
degraded: true Re-check
The provider did not answer. The response says nothing about the address: it is not a rejection. Run the check again later.
Integrations
No Shopify app, no WooCommerce extension: the call is made from your own service.
What we keep, and what we do not. In our database, a paid or subscribed call leaves only a fingerprint of the address (cache_key) and the outcome; the free trial without an account leaves nothing, only an anonymous counter that enforces the cap. The cache keeps each result (the address in standard form, its coordinates and the verdict) for {{cache_jours}} days under that fingerprint, then deletes it. A deletion request is made with the cache_key.
