How it works

One request, three steps.

Our API standardizes the street, city and ZIP code, locates the address and says whether it is deliverable. A doubtful address is fixed before it ships.

  1. 01

    Send the address

    Call POST /validate with the street, city, state, ZIP code and country, from your back office, an order webhook or a spreadsheet.

    "street": "100 stewart st"
  2. 02

    Receive the validation

    The standardized address, its GPS coordinates, its deliverability and a confidence score from 0 to 1.

    "deliverability": "residential"
  3. 03

    Ship the corrected address

    Doubtful addresses are corrected or set aside before they go to the carrier. That is where the return is avoided.

    "line1": "100 Stewart Street"

Frequently asked questions

Before you subscribe.

Another question? Write to {{contact}}

Do I need a credit card for the free trial?

No. The trial lasts {{jours_essai}} days and includes {{validations_essai}} validations, with no credit card and no commitment. At the end, you pick a plan, or nothing happens.

What happens if I go over my quota?

The quota is monthly and resets on the 1st of the month (UTC). Once it is reached, the API answers 402 with quota_atteint: nothing extra is billed and no automatic charge takes place. Your usage is visible in your dashboard. To move up to the next plan, write to us.

What happens to the addresses I send you?

They are used for validation. The cache keeps the address in standard form for {{jours_cache}} days, filed under a SHA-256 fingerprint, then deletes it. Nothing is resold or used for advertising.

Do you cover Réunion and international addresses?

United States addresses are optimised (USPS DPV); France and overseas territories are verified by the Base Adresse Nationale; addresses from other countries are accepted, but verification there stays partial: a response is not a full confirmation. The known limits are published in the API manifest.

How long does integration take?

A first call takes a few minutes: POST /validate with your key in the X-API-Key header. To connect it to your orders, the guide covers the direct call, CSV import, Shopify and WooCommerce, with code examples. There is no Shopify app to install: the call is made from your own service.

GDPR compliance. The addresses you send are used only for validation and are neither resold nor used for advertising. The cache keeps the address in standard form for {{jours_cache}} days, filed under a SHA-256 fingerprint, then deletes it. Operating logs pseudonymize the IP address (SHA-256 and salt) and are purged after {{jours_journaux}} days. You can request deletion of your account data at any time.
